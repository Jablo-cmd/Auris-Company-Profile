## PART A — PHOTOGRAPHY

## 1. Position

**Documentary, not aspirational.**

The category default is aspirational stock: diverse models in glass boardrooms, laughing at
laptops, pointing at screens nobody is looking at. Every reader in the nine target audiences has
seen these images in a hundred profiles. They carry no information, and their presence signals that
the company had nothing real to show.

This publication uses photography as **evidence**. Sixteen photographic positions across 112 pages —
deliberately restrained. Where there is nothing true to photograph, the page uses white space, a
diagram or the apex motif instead. An honest empty page is stronger than a dishonest full one.

---

## 2. Art direction

| Attribute | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Light** | Natural, directional, single dominant source. Window light preferred. No ring lights, no beauty dish, no on-camera flash |
| **Colour** | Cool-neutral grade, restrained saturation. Blues and greys hold; skin tones stay true and are never pushed warm |
| **Depth of field** | Shallow but not extreme — f/2.8 to f/4. The subject separates; the environment stays readable |
| **Framing** | Wide enough to show context. A person is always shown *somewhere*, doing something |
| **Posture** | Working, not posing. Mid-action, mid-conversation, mid-thought. Eye contact with the camera only in formal executive portraits |
| **Environment** | Real workplaces, real client environments, real equipment. Nothing staged in a rented boardroom |
| **Composition** | Off-centre subject on a thirds intersection, generous negative space on the side where type will sit |
| **Grade** | Consistent LUT across all images so sixteen photographs read as one commission |
| **Aspect** | Shot at 3:2, cropped in the layout. Never up-scaled |
| **Resolution** | Minimum 300 dpi at placed size; 4000 px longest edge for full-bleed positions |

---

## 3. Subject matter

### Photograph

- People working: at a planning wall, in a workshop, reviewing a screen with a colleague
- Hands and detail: a whiteboard mid-diagram, a notebook, cabling, a rack, a device under test
- Real environments: the office as it is, a client site, a data centre, a site visit
- Executive portraits: environmental, in the working space, natural light
- South African context: recognisable built environment, landscape and infrastructure on part dividers

### Never photograph, and never licence

- Handshakes of any kind
- Jigsaw pieces, chess pieces, ladders, ascending arrows, dartboards, lightbulb-over-head
- Glowing brains, circuit-board globes, blue binary rain, "cyber" padlocks in space
- Hands touching floating holographic interfaces
- Groups of people in a boardroom laughing at a laptop
- Anyone pointing at a screen or a chart
- Rows of identical smiling faces, arms folded
- Server rooms with unrealistic blue accent lighting
- Anything containing legible fictitious data, fake dashboards or invented client names

The prohibition list is not stylistic fastidiousness. Each of these images is a recognised marker of
a low-budget profile, and a procurement reviewer who has seen forty submissions reads them
accordingly.

## 4. Treatment

| Treatment | Specification | Applied to |
|---|---|---|
| **Natural** | Graded, no overlay | Editorial images, executive portraits, case studies |
| **Navy duotone** | Nexus Navy shadows → Signal Cyan highlights | Part divider imagery only |
| **Navy scrim** | Nexus Navy at 70% over image | Any position where type overlays photography |
| **Apex overlay** | Apex silhouette in Apex Field `#002C56` (solid), composited into the image | Cover and Part Two/Three dividers only |

**All treatments are composited into the image in Photoshop and delivered flattened as CMYK TIFF.**
No scrim, duotone or overlay is applied as live transparency in the layout — see
`10_Print-Production.md` §4.2.

**Rule:** type is never set directly over an ungraded photograph. Either a 70% scrim or a solid
panel intervenes. Contrast of type over any treated image is verified to 4.5 : 1 minimum at
Stage 12.

## 5. Consent, rights and licensing

This section is a compliance requirement, not a production note.

| Requirement | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Employee images** | Written model release for every identifiable person, retained on file. Consent covers print, digital and derivative editions, and states the retention period |
| **Client premises** | Written permission before any photography on a client site. Client-identifying material — signage, screens, documents — removed or obscured unless separately consented |
| **Screens and data** | No real client data visible in any photograph, ever. Screens are either blank, showing a neutral interface, or obscured. This is a POPIA obligation, not a preference |
| **Stock licensing** | Extended or enhanced licence required for print runs over 500,000 impressions and for cover use. Standard licences frequently exclude both |
| **Attribution** | Licence references for all stock recorded in `03_Images/licence-register.md` and summarised in the document control block |
| **AI-generated imagery** | **Prohibited** in this publication. Rights provenance is unresolved, several public-sector procurement policies now require disclosure, and an AI-generated "team" photograph in a credentials document is a misrepresentation |

Where consent or a suitable real photograph cannot be obtained, the position is filled with a
diagram, a fact panel or white space. It is never filled with stock standing in for reality.

| # | Page | Subject | Treatment | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | 12 | Office or founding-era establishing shot | Natural | Own archive |

| 4 | 23 | Part Two divider — hands at work, detail | Navy duotone | Commissioned |

| 13 | 43 | Part Three divider — South African built environment | Navy duotone | Licensed |

**Commissioning recommendation.** Twelve of the sixteen positions require original photography. A
single one-day shoot with a corporate documentary photographer covers positions 1–5, 14 and 15, and
costs materially less than extended stock licences for equivalent coverage — while producing images
no competitor can hold. This is the highest-return budget line in the project.

## PART B — ILLUSTRATION

## 7. Position

Illustration is used **only** where a photograph cannot show the subject: abstract concepts,
system relationships, and the apex and orbit motifs. It is never used as decoration and never used as a
substitute for a photograph that could have been taken.

## 8. Style

| Attribute | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Form** | Geometric line-work. Constructed from circles, rectangles and orthogonal paths — the same vocabulary as the diagram system |
| **Stroke** | 1 pt uniform, Auris Cyan or Orbit Deep. No variable stroke weight |
| **Angle** | Diagonals constructed at 3 : 5 (31°), the governing angle. No other angle is used |
| **Fill** | Flat, single tint from the tint ladder. No gradients, textures or noise |
| **Perspective** | Orthographic only. No vanishing points, no isometric "3D" scenes |
| **Figures** | **No human figures.** Illustration in this system depicts systems, not people — people are photographed |
| **Complexity** | Maximum 12 distinct elements per illustration |

## 9. Prohibited illustration styles

- **Corporate Memphis** — flat figures with elongated limbs and oversized hands. The single most
  dated visual signal in current corporate publishing
- Isometric city-scapes and isometric "technology stacks"
- Hand-drawn or "sketch" styles, including deliberate wobble
- Gradient mesh, glassmorphism, neumorphism
- Three-dimensional rendered objects — floating phones, servers, shields
- Any illustration with a drop shadow

## 10. Production

| Requirement | Specification |
|---|---|
| Format | SVG, editable paths |
| Colour | Palette names only, applied as `currentColor` or named theme colours |
| Word implementation | Placed as SVG, convertible to native shapes |
| Storage | `03_Images/illustration/` |
| Naming | `an-illus-<subject>.svg` |

---

## 11. Image handling in the document

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Working file | 16-bit **Adobe RGB (1998)**, layered PSD — wider gamut than sRGB for the CMYK conversion |
| Delivery format | **CMYK TIFF**, 8-bit, LZW, flattened, no alpha, no layers, FOGRA39 embedded |
| Print resolution | **300 dpi at placed size, minimum.** Line art 1200 dpi or vector |
| Screen PDF | Derived separately at 150 dpi, sRGB, JPEG quality 80 |
| Ink limit | 300% TAC, verified per image |
| Shadow / highlight | No channel below 5% or above 95% in significant areas — shadows plug and highlights blow out on press |
| Sharpening | Output sharpening applied **after** resampling to final size, tuned for coated stock |
| Upsampling | **Never.** An image below requirement is replaced, recropped smaller, or the layout changes |

### Pixel requirements at 300 dpi

| Layout | Placed size | Minimum pixels |
|---|---|---|
| L1 — Full bleed, single page | 216 × 303 mm | **2551 × 3579** |
| L1s — Full bleed, spread | 426 × 303 mm | **5031 × 3579** |
| L2 — Band | 170 × 90 mm | **2008 × 1063** |
| L3 — Half | 83 × 110 mm | **980 × 1299** |
| L4 — Quarter | 54 × 45 mm | **638 × 531** |
| L5 — Inset / portrait | 54 × 68 mm | **638 × 803** |
| Corners | Square. **No rounded corners on any image** |
| Borders | None. Images sit on white or bleed. No keylines, no frames |
| Captions | Where an image carries information. Decorative images are uncaptioned — and if an image is decorative, its presence should be questioned |
| Alt text | Required on every image in the Word master and tagged PDF, for accessibility compliance |
