# Project Charter
## Auris Nexus Technologies (Pty) Ltd — Corporate Profile

**Status:** Active rebuild  
**Working date:** 23 September 2026  
**Master content:** `02_Content/00_Master-Company-Profile.md`

## 1. Purpose

The Auris Nexus Corporate Profile is the company's primary commercial and institutional introduction. It must make a reader confident about six things:

1. Who Auris Nexus is.
2. What Auris Nexus builds and supports.
3. Where its capabilities are relevant.
4. What evidence demonstrates those capabilities.
5. How engagements are delivered and governed.
6. How a prospective client, funder, partner or procurement team can take the next step.

This is a **business-development document with procurement-grade discipline**. It is not a catalogue of services and it is not a substitute for formal compliance documents.

## 2. Commercial objectives

| Objective | What the profile must achieve |
|---|---|
| Credibility | Present Auris as a serious South African technology business without overstating scale or maturity. |
| Conversion | Make it easy for a decision-maker to understand the problem Auris solves and start a conversation. |
| Evidence | Use real products, platforms, technical work and company credentials as proof. |
| Procurement readiness | Surface legal identity, ownership, B-BBEE, CSD and governance information clearly. |
| Partnership readiness | Explain how Auris can build, modernise, automate, integrate and support technology. |
| Reuse | Provide a controlled source for capability statements, tender submissions, sector profiles and presentations. |

## 3. Primary audiences

- Corporate and enterprise decision-makers
- Procurement and supplier-development teams
- Government and institutional buyers
- Schools and education organisations
- Potential strategic partners
- Funders and development organisations
- Prospective technology collaborators

The document should work for both a five-minute executive scan and a deeper technical/procurement review.

## 4. Master publication scope

The previous 112-page architecture is retired. The master profile is now designed around **approximately 55–65 pages**, with final pagination determined after copy, evidence and artwork are assembled.

The master should contain:

- Front cover and company at a glance
- Company overview and positioning
- Purpose, vision and working principles
- Solution portfolio
- Business contexts and sectors
- Selected work and platforms
- Technology and engineering capability
- Security, privacy and responsible engineering
- Delivery approach
- Business excellence and governance
- Credentials
- Engagement and contact

The profile should be long enough to establish confidence, but short enough to be read.

## 5. Governing narrative

Every major section should follow, where relevant:

**Business problem → Solution → Engineering → Evidence → Business value → Partnership**

This is the central editorial test. If a page does not help the reader understand one of those elements, it should justify its place or be removed.

## 6. Evidence rules

All statements are classified as:

- **Delivered** — supported by an identifiable Auris project, platform or artefact.
- **Current capability** — Auris can deliver this now.
- **Available capability** — capability can be assembled or delivered where required.
- **Planned** — future capability, clearly labelled.

The profile must not invent:

- project counts
- customer counts
- revenue
- retention
- uptime
- integration counts
- certifications
- memberships
- client logos
- testimonials
- formal SLAs
- regulatory approvals
- outcomes that have not been measured

Where evidence is unavailable, the copy uses capability language rather than pretending a result exists.

## 10. Deliverables

The master profile becomes the source for:

- Executive Profile
- Capability Statement
- Tender / procurement short-form
- Sector-specific profiles
- Presentation content
- Website company/about copy

Derivatives may be shortened or rearranged, but material factual claims must remain controlled by the master content and Fact Register.

## 11. Production principle

The profile should feel premium through:

- strong hierarchy
- disciplined typography
- purposeful diagrams
- meaningful evidence
- restrained use of colour
- clean tables
- real project visuals
- generous white space

It must not depend on decorative complexity or exaggerated language to appear impressive.
