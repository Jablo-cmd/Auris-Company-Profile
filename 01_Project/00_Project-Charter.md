## 1. Purpose of the publication

The Corporate Profile is the company's primary written instrument of credibility. It exists to
answer, in a single controlled document, the four questions every institutional buyer asks before
awarding work:

1. **Who are you?** — legal standing, ownership, governance, longevity.
2. **What can you actually do?** — capability, not aspiration.
3. **Have you done it before?** — evidence, references, delivery record.
4. **What is the risk of appointing you?** — compliance, continuity, quality, security.

A profile that answers only the first two is marketing. A profile that answers all four is a
procurement asset. This publication is built as the latter.

## 3. Audiences and what each one reads

The document must serve nine audiences with sharply different reading behaviour. It is designed so
that each can enter at a different point and leave satisfied.

| Audience | Reading time | Entry point | Primary decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Government departments | 5–10 min, checklist-driven | Credentials, compliance, B-BBEE | Does this bidder meet mandatory requirements? |
| State-owned enterprises | 10–20 min | Delivery methodology, governance | Can they operate inside our controls? |
| Municipalities | 5 min | Capability statement, contact | Are they credible and reachable? |
| Banks | 20–30 min, adversarial | Information security, POPIA, BCM | Would appointing them create risk? |
| Insurance companies | 15 min | Data, integration, analytics | Can they modernise our systems? |
| Universities | 10 min | Education sector, partnerships | Do they understand our environment? |
| Enterprise clients | 15–25 min | Services, industries, case studies | Are they the right fit for this problem? |
| International organisations | 10 min | Governance, standards alignment | Do they meet international norms? |
| Investors / partners | 20 min | Overview, operating model, statistics | Is this a scalable, well-run business? |

**Design consequence:** the publication uses a three-layer reading structure (see
`01_Publication-Strategy.md` §4) so a 5-minute reader and a 30-minute reader both succeed.

## 4. Scope

### In scope
- One master publication (A4 portrait, **112 pages**), composed for commercial offset press.
- Complete design system, reusable across all future company collateral.
- Press-ready PDF/X-4, screen PDF and tender PDF outputs.
- An editable Word (`.docx`) derivative with native styles, fields and auto-generated contents.
- All diagrams and tables as native, editable objects in the Word derivative — never flattened images.
- Three derivative editions cut from the same source (see §5).

### Also in scope, following the press-first requirement
- Press-ready artwork: PDF/X-4, FOGRA39, bleed, marks, preflight.
- CMYK colour management and gamut verification of the full palette.
- Stock, finishing and binding specification for the printer.

### Out of scope for this project
- Logo design or corporate identity redesign. The profile *applies* the existing identity;
  if no formal identity manual exists, this document establishes one by precedent.
- Website, social or campaign collateral.
- Photography commissioning and shoots (art direction, shot lists and technical specification are provided; production is not).
- Print buying, printer selection and press attendance (specification is provided; procurement is not).
- Vector rebuild of the logo (specification is provided; execution is a separate design commission).
- Legal review of compliance claims. All regulatory statements require sign-off by the company.

| **Capability Statement** | 2 pp | Single-sheet qualification response | Extracted from master |

All four editions share one design system, one voice, one fact base. Derivatives are *cut*, never
*rewritten* — this is what keeps a document set consistent over time and is the single most common
failure point in corporate publications.

## 6. Governing principles

These are binding constraints on every subsequent stage.

**P1 — Evidence over adjective.**
Every claim is either verifiable, attributed, or removed. "Leading provider" is not a claim; it is
an opinion wearing a claim's clothing. Where the company is young or the record is short, the
document says so and converts it into a strength (agility, senior-only teams, direct principal
access) rather than concealing it.

**P2 — No fabricated credentials.**
Registration numbers, tax status, B-BBEE level, certifications, client names, testimonials and
awards are **never** invented, estimated or illustrated with plausible-looking specimens. Unknowns
are carried as explicit tokens (`[[BBBEE_LEVEL]]`) until the company supplies them. In a tender
context an unverifiable credential is not an embellishment — it is grounds for disqualification and,
under the Public Finance Management Act and the Preferential Procurement regulations, potential
restriction from future bidding.

**P3 — Aspiration must be labelled.**
Capabilities the company intends to build are presented under headings that say so —
"Certification Roadmap", "Planned Capability" — never blended into present-tense capability claims.

**P4 — Press-first, editable in derivative.**
Commercial sheet-fed offset is the **primary** output medium. The publication is conceived, composed
and colour-managed for press, and is deliverable to a printer with minimal redesign. Editability is
not sacrificed to achieve this: diagrams, tables and charts are authored once as vector and deployed
twice — as press artwork in the master, and as native Word objects in the client-editable
derivative. Nothing a client may need to update is delivered to them as a flattened bitmap.

**P5 — Survives the recipient's environment, in every medium.**
On press: within FOGRA39 gamut, under 300% ink, no live transparency, no rule below press minimum.
On screen and in Word: renders correctly on an unremarkable government or corporate PC with no brand
fonts installed. On a photocopier: every part colour holds 10 points of greyscale separation. Font
fallback, greyscale behaviour and a mono-safe variant are designed for, not hoped for.

**P6 — Publication, not brochure.**
The finished document must read as a professionally art-directed book — comparable to a premium
annual report or investment prospectus — not as a corporate brochure or a word-processed file. Every
page is composed; none is filled.

**P7 — One voice.**
A single register is maintained end to end: measured, precise, senior. See
`01_Publication-Strategy.md` §5 for the voice specification and the proscribed-language list.

## 7. Constraints and risks

| # | Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Company fact base not yet supplied | Blocks Stages 3, 9 and final assembly | Fact Register (`05_Fact-Register.md`) issued at Stage 1; tokens carried until resolved |
| R2 | No existing brand identity manual | Design system may conflict with future identity work | Stage 1 palette and type system designed to be identity-compatible; single-file change point if logo colours differ |
| R3 | Thin verifiable delivery record | Case studies and testimonials sections weak | Structure supports anonymised engagements and capability demonstrations; placeholders clearly marked |
| R4 | Compliance claims made without evidence | Tender disqualification, reputational damage | P2 above; all regulatory content flagged for company sign-off at Stage 12 |
| R5 | Brand fonts unavailable to recipients | Document reflows, looks unprofessional | Embedded fonts in PDF; embedding enabled in Word; system-safe fallback matrix in `07_Branding/03_Typography.md` §3 |
| R7 | **Logo supplied as raster only** | **Cannot go to press.** No transparency for navy fields, no vector edges, gradient will band | Vector rebuild commissioned against the measurements in `07_Branding/08_Logo-and-Identity.md` §1. **Blocker for Stage 11** |
| R8 | Dual press/Word output doubles diagram and table production | Schedule overrun at Stages 10–11 | Costed and accepted: ~25–30% additional production time. See `07_Branding/10_Print-Production.md` §1.1 |
| R6 | Scope drift across twelve stages | Inconsistent voice and design | Stage-gate approval; design system frozen after Stage 2 sign-off |

## 8. Method

Twelve sequential stages, each gated by explicit approval. No stage begins before its predecessor is
approved. At each gate the deliverable is reviewed for internal consistency, consistency with all
prior stages, and fitness for the procurement purpose.

**Freeze points:**
- After **Stage 2** — the design system is frozen. Later changes are change-controlled, because a
  component altered at Stage 9 invalidates every page built between Stages 3 and 8.
- After **Stage 9** — the fact base is frozen. Content may be edited for style thereafter, but no
  new factual claims enter the document without returning to the Fact Register.
