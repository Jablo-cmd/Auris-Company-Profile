# Information Architecture
## Auris Nexus Technologies (Pty) Ltd — Corporate Profile

**Status:** Active rebuild  
**Master source:** `02_Content/00_Master-Company-Profile.md`

## 1. Architecture principle

The profile is organised around the buyer's questions rather than internal departments.

**Company → Solutions → Context → Evidence → Engineering → Delivery → Excellence → Credentials → Engage**

The section is the main reusable content unit. Each section should remain understandable when extracted for a proposal, presentation or short-form capability document.

## 2. Master structure

| Part | Title | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Front | Auris Nexus | Establish identity and immediate credibility |
| 1 | The Company | Who Auris is, what it stands for and how it works |
| 2 | Solutions | What Auris can build and deliver |
| 3 | Business Contexts | Where the capability is relevant |
| 4 | Selected Work | Evidence through real platforms and engagements |
| 5 | Engineering Capability | Technology, architecture, data, integration and responsible engineering |
| 6 | Delivery & Partnership | How Auris works from discovery through support |
| 7 | Business Excellence | Quality, governance, security, privacy and continuous improvement |
| 8 | Credentials & Evidence | Registration, ownership, B-BBEE, CSD, governance status and evidence register |
| Closing | Engage Auris Nexus | Clear next step and contact |

## 11. Part 7 — Business Excellence

Cover:

- governance

## 12. Part 8 — Credentials & Evidence

- B-BBEE Level 1
- CSD registration
- Head office
- Current certification status
- Membership status
- Governance-policy status
- Evidence / due-diligence note

Open items remain visibly controlled rather than filled with assumptions.
