# 7.11 Client recognition

**Standfirst**

> open rather than represented by unattributed or invented praise.

---

## Copy

**Testimonials:** [[TESTIMONIALS]] — none confirmed with named attribution and consent at this stage.
Per Fact Register **F3**, a testimonial is only used here with the client's name, role, organisation
and explicit consent recorded — an anonymous or unattributed quotation is not used as a substitute,
because it cannot be verified by a reader and reads as invented even when it is not.

**Awards and recognition:** [[AWARDS]] — none confirmed. See Fact Register **F4**.

The two named, consented case studies in 7.10 — Jo Jackson Dance Company and Pro Energy Solutions — are
the closest this profile comes to third-party recognition, and their consent covers naming and
describing the work only, not a quoted endorsement. No quote is attributed to either business without
separate, specific consent to do so.
