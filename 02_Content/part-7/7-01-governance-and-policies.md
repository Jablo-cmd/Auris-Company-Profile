# 7.1 Governance and Business Integrity

Auris Nexus maintains a governance foundation appropriate to a technology business handling client information and delivering business-critical software.

The company has developed policies covering areas including:

- POPIA and privacy
- Information security
- Complaints and compliments
- Health and safety
- Procurement and suppliers
- Anti-bribery and corruption
- Business continuity
- Code of conduct and ethics
- Data retention and records management
- Conflict of interest

Policy approval and adoption status is controlled through the company's Governance Adoption Record. A policy should not be presented as formally adopted until the required approval has been completed.

## Responsible business

Auris Nexus is committed to professional conduct, transparent commercial relationships, protection of information and responsible treatment of clients, suppliers and other stakeholders.

The governance framework is designed to support growth without sacrificing accountability.
