# 7.6 Certification roadmap

**Layout:** Archetype F. Future-tense, explicitly labelled — this page describes intent, not a
current or in-progress credential.

---

## Copy

**Standfirst**

> No formal certification roadmap has yet been set, with target dates, for Auris Nexus Technologies.
> This page is left honestly open rather than populated with plausible-sounding targets.

---

### Copy

Certifications such as ISO 27001 (information security management) or ISO 9001 (quality management)
are common targets for a growing technology company, and Auris Nexus may pursue them as it grows
beyond its current single-founder structure. **No specific certification target, with a realistic
date, has been set at this stage** — see [[CERTS_PLANNED]]. Stating a target date without a genuine
commitment behind it would misrepresent this page's own explicit purpose, which is to show planning
rigour, not aspiration.

Where a specific certification is later targeted with a genuine, realistic timeline, it will be
stated here with the certifying body and target date, following the same evidence discipline as the
rest of this document.
