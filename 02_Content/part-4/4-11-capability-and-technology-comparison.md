# 4.11 Capability and technology comparison

**Layout:** Archetype G, full-width table page, two tables.

---

## Copy

**Standfirst**

> Ten domains, stated plainly rather than uniformly. A capability section that rates every domain
> equally is not being assessed — it is being marketed.

Self-rated maturity claims are only credible when the rating is not uniformly favourable — a domain
list where every entry scores at the top of its scale reads as promotional rather than assessed, and
experienced evaluators discount it accordingly. The maturity table below is populated only once a
genuine, differentiated self-assessment exists; it is not populated with placeholder high ratings in
the interim.
