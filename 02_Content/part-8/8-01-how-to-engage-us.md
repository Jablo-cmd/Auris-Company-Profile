# 8.1 How to engage us

**Standfirst**

> A short, practical answer to the question this document exists to prompt: what happens next if you
> want to appoint us.

---

## Copy

Auris Nexus Technologies can be engaged through open tender, request for quotation, panel or framework
appointment, and direct appointment where regulations permit (1.2, 3.7). Five commercial models are
available — fixed price, time and materials, managed service, retained advisory, and outcome-based
engagement (2.9) — and the right one depends on how well the requirement is understood before work
begins, not on a default preference.

For a tender or formal procurement process, direct any bid-related correspondence to the contact given
in 8.2. **Tender and bid desk contact:** [[TENDER_CONTACT]].

For a direct enquiry or an initial conversation about a requirement, the founder is the first point of
contact for every engagement, consistent with the operating model described in 1.10 — there is no
separate sales function to route a first conversation through.
