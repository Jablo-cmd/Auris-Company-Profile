# 6.8 Innovation

**Standfirst**

> Innovation is positioned here as a capability area the company is organised to apply, not as a claim
> of specific delivered outcomes beyond what Part Four and Part Seven evidence.

---

## Copy

**AI & Emerging Technology Integration** is one of the five capability areas set out in 1.10 —
practical application and integration of AI and emerging technologies where they provide an
appropriate business benefit, applied with the same discipline described in 4.7: no maturity claim, no
specific vendor or model named, and human review retained wherever a decision carries material
consequence for a person.

This is stated as a **capability area the company is positioned to offer**, not as a record of AI
projects already delivered — the two are genuinely different claims, and this profile does not let one
stand in for the other. Where AI or an emerging technology has actually been applied in a delivered
system, that fact is stated in the relevant technical domain (4.7) or case study (7.10) directly, with
its own evidence; nothing broader is implied by this page.

**Responsible-use governance policy:** [[AI_GOVERNANCE_POLICY]] — a documented policy, once it exists,
is referenced here rather than the philosophy above being treated as the policy itself, consistent
with 4.7's own note on this token.

More broadly, the "prefer subtraction" and "understand before recommending" discipline stated in 1.6
and 1.7 governs how innovation is approached across every capability area, not only the AI-specific
one: a new technology is adopted because it solves a stated problem better than the alternative, not
because it is new.
