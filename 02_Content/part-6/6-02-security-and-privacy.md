# 6.2 Security, Privacy and Responsible Engineering

Security is part of building a business system properly.

Where relevant to the engagement, Auris Nexus considers:

- Authentication and secure access
- Role-based permissions
- Tenant isolation
- Data-layer access controls
- Secure handling of sensitive information
- Auditability
- Dependency and configuration management
- Backup and recovery considerations
- Secure deployment practices
- Privacy-conscious system design

South African privacy requirements, including POPIA, are considered where personal information is processed.

Auris Nexus does not present itself as ISO-certified or as a specialist cybersecurity consultancy unless a specific credential or engagement establishes that position. Security controls described in this profile are engineering capabilities, not independent certification.
