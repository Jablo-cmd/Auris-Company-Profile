# 6.1 Quality and Delivery Governance

Auris Nexus uses practical delivery controls to keep projects aligned with agreed requirements.

Depending on scope, these include:

- Defined requirements and priorities
- Solution and workflow design
- Role and permission definition
- Version-controlled development
- Testing and review
- Controlled deployment
- Documentation
- Client review points
- Issue and change tracking
- Post-launch maintenance and enhancement

These controls are adapted to the size and risk of the engagement. Auris Nexus does not claim a formal ISO-certified quality-management system or PMO unless separately evidenced.

## A disciplined delivery relationship

The client should know:

**What is being built → why it is being built → what has been completed → what remains → what decisions are required.**

That visibility is central to responsible technology delivery.
