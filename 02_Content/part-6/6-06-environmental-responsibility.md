# 6.6 Environmental responsibility

**Standfirst**

> A software company's direct environmental footprint is small and this section says so plainly,
> rather than inflating routine office practice into a claimed environmental programme.

---

## Copy

Auris Nexus Technologies' direct environmental footprint is that of a small, office-based technology
company — it does not operate plant, fleet or manufacturing facilities of the kind described in Part
Three's industrial and resources treatment (3.4). Software delivery's environmental impact is
concentrated in the compute resources it relies on, and the company selects cloud and hosting
infrastructure per the technology decisions described in 4.3, without a specific claim of carbon
measurement or offsetting attached to those choices at this stage.

A formal environmental policy or documented sustainability commitment is not yet in place. This is
stated directly rather than substituted with general environmental language that would not reflect an
actual, adopted policy. As the company grows and its infrastructure footprint grows with it, adopting
a documented environmental policy is a reasonable future step, not a current claim.

## Production notes

- **First drafted 2026-09-13, Stage 9.** Kept short and proportionate — a lengthy environmental
