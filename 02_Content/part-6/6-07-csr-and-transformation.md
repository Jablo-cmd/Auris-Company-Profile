# 6.7 Corporate social responsibility and transformation

**Standfirst**

> One transformation credential is genuinely confirmed and stated directly. Corporate social
> responsibility activity beyond it is not yet formalised, and is not invented to fill the page.

---

## Copy

**Transformation.** Auris Nexus Technologies holds a **B-BBEE Level 1** contribution status. The
verification agency, certificate number and expiry date are not yet supplied and are not stated here
in their absence — the level itself is the confirmed fact; the supporting certificate detail belongs
in 7.4 once available. As a 100%-owned, newly established company, Auris Nexus's transformation profile
is straightforward to state honestly rather than requiring elaboration.

**Skills development.** Formal learnership, internship or structured skills-development activity is
not yet in place at the company's current size. This is stated directly rather than implied through
general transformation language that would not reflect an actual, running programme.

**Corporate social responsibility.** Specific CSR activity — as distinct from the transformation
credential above — is not yet established as an ongoing, evidenced practice. As the company and its
delivery capacity grow, formalising a CSR commitment is a reasonable future step, stated here as
intent rather than as current activity.
