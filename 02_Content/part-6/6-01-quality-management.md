# 6.1 Quality management

**Standfirst**

> Quality is treated here as an engineering and delivery discipline, evidenced in Part Four and Part
> Five, not as a certified management system the company does not yet hold.

---

## Copy

Quality management, for Auris Nexus at its current stage, is a set of practices applied directly by
the founder to every engagement, not a separate function reviewing someone else's work — see 1.10 for
why that distinction is stated plainly rather than implied away.

What is genuinely evidenced: automated testing is a demonstrated practice, not an assumed one, in the
Auris-developed systems described in 4.1 — unit and component testing, and in two of three inspected
systems, end-to-end browser testing, confirmed by direct source inspection rather than asserted.
Section 5.4 describes quality assurance as an explicit phase in the delivery lifecycle, run as its own
step rather than folded silently into functional testing.

What is not yet formalised, and is not claimed here: a documented quality management system, a
specific coverage threshold policy, or a quality certification such as ISO 9001. **Certifications
currently held:** [[CERTS_HELD]] — none confirmed at this stage. **Code quality regime detail beyond
what 4.1 evidences:** [[CODE_QUALITY_REGIME]].

As the company grows beyond its current single-person structure, formalising a documented quality
management approach — rather than a discipline applied personally by the founder — is the intended
next step, consistent with the operating-model note in 1.10.
