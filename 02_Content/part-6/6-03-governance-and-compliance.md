# 6.3 Corporate governance and compliance

**Standfirst**

> Governance appropriate to a newly established, sole-director private company — not a corporate
> governance framework borrowed from a listed entity it does not resemble.

---

## Copy

Auris Nexus Technologies is a private company incorporated in South Africa, with a single director —
the founder, Loyiso Ngcala (7.2). Governance obligations that apply to a company of this form and
size — statutory record-keeping, tax registration, and compliance with the Companies Act requirements
applicable to a private company — are the governance baseline this section describes, rather than a
board structure, committees, or an independent oversight function the company does not have.

This is a deliberate departure from how larger suppliers describe governance, and it is stated
directly per the correction made in 1.10 and 5.8: this profile does not claim an independent
governance layer distinct from the person delivering the work, because at one employee, no such
separation exists. What does exist is direct, undistributed accountability — the founder decides,
delivers and answers for every engagement personally.

A formal code of conduct, anti-corruption policy, or conflict-of-interest policy is not yet
separately documented. As the company grows, formalising these in writing — rather than relying on the
founder's direct, personal accountability — is the intended next step.
