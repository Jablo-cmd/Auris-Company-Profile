---

### Copy

Business excellence sections in a company profile are usually written for an organisation with
dedicated functions behind each heading — a quality manager, a risk committee, a health and safety
representative. Auris Nexus is a newly established, one-person company, and this Part is written
honestly against that fact rather than borrowing language that implies functions which do not yet
exist.

The eight areas that follow — quality management, risk and business continuity, governance and
compliance, POPIA and information security, occupational health and safety, environmental
responsibility, corporate social responsibility and transformation, and innovation — are treated
consistently: what the company actually does today is stated as fact; what is intended as the company
grows is stated as intent; and what has not yet been formalised is stated as an open item, not
implied.

Two things are already genuinely evidenced and recur through this Part: a Level 1 B-BBEE contribution
status (7.4), and the security-by-design engineering discipline already demonstrated in the
tenant-isolation and row-level-security work described in Part Four (4.4, 4.6) and the case studies in
7.10. Neither is repeated here beyond a cross-reference.
