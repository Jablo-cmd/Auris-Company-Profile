# 6.5 Occupational health and safety

**Standfirst**

> A software company's health and safety exposure is not comparable to a site-based operation's, and
> this section is scaled to the actual nature of the work rather than a generic template.

---

## Copy

Auris Nexus Technologies delivers software and technology consulting work, carried out from an office
environment rather than a site, plant or field operation. The health and safety exposure relevant to
this kind of work is materially different from the industrial and site-based contexts described
elsewhere in this document (3.4), and this section is scaled accordingly rather than restating generic
occupational health and safety language that does not reflect the actual nature of the work.

A formal, documented health and safety policy and an appointed representative, as would typically be
expected of a larger or site-based employer, are not yet in place at the company's current size. This
is stated directly rather than implied to exist through generic assurance language.

Where an engagement requires Auris Nexus personnel to work at a client's premises or site — for
example, discovery sessions in the industrial, security or field-services contexts described in Part
Three — the client's own site health and safety requirements are followed, consistent with standard
practice for a visiting contractor.

## Production notes

- **First drafted 2026-09-13, Stage 9.** Kept deliberately short and scaled to the company's actual
  size and nature of work — a lengthy formal-sounding policy statement for a one-person office-based
  consultancy would read as padding, not substance.
- Do not claim a formal, documented health and safety policy or appointed representative exists unless
  the owner separately confirms it — see Fact Register **G4**.
