## ① Service title

Web Design and Development
*Services covered: website design & development · search engine optimisation*

---

## ② Standfirst (40–55 words)

> A website is frequently an organisation's only unattended representative — open when the office is
> not, answering the questions a visitor did not think to phone in about. We design and build it, then
> make sure it can be found.

---

## ③ Description

Website Design and Development covers the site itself — information architecture, visual design
consistent with the organisation's identity, content structure, and a technical build that performs
under real traffic rather than only in a demonstration. We design for the visitor's task, not the
organisation's org chart, which is the most common structural error in institutional websites: a
navigation that mirrors internal departments rather than what a visitor actually came to do.

Search Engine Optimisation is treated as part of the same discipline, not a separate add-on purchased
later. A well-built site that cannot be found by the people searching for what it offers has not
achieved its purpose, and retrofitting SEO onto a site not built to support it is materially more
expensive than building it in from the information architecture stage.

---

## ④ Problems this solves

- The current website does not reflect the organisation's current identity, services or credibility
- Visitors cannot complete their actual task on the site — finding a contact, a service, a document —
  because the structure follows the organisation rather than the visitor
- The site is not found in search results for the terms the organisation should rank for
- The site performs poorly on mobile devices or under real (not demonstration) traffic conditions

---

## ⑤ Business benefits

- A site that functions as a credible, always-available point of first contact
- Reduced enquiry load on staff for information the site should answer directly
- Improved discoverability for the searches that matter to the organisation's actual audience
- A technical build that performs consistently rather than only in a controlled demonstration

---

## ⑥ Typical deliverables

- Information architecture and content strategy
- Visual design aligned to brand identity
- Responsive, accessible front-end build
- Content management system configuration and training
- On-page and technical SEO implementation
- Performance and security baseline testing
- Analytics and search console configuration
- Launch support and post-launch monitoring period
