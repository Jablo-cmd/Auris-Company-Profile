# 2.9 Engagement and commercial models

**Layout:** Archetype G, full-width table page.

---

## Copy

**Standfirst**

> How we contract shapes who carries risk and when. We offer five models, named plainly rather than
> dressed up, so a client can choose the one that fits the certainty they actually have.

The right model depends on how well the requirement is understood before work begins. A precisely
scoped requirement suits fixed price. A requirement that will genuinely evolve suits time and
materials, honestly priced rather than fixed price with scope creep absorbed informally. Ongoing
operation suits a managed service. Strategic input without a build suits retained advisory. Where an
outcome can be defined and measured directly, outcome-based engagement aligns our commercial interest
with the client's result rather than with hours worked.

---

## Table 2.1 — Engagement models

| Model | How it works | Risk allocation | Best suited to | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fixed price** | Scope, price and timeline agreed before work begins; changes go through formal change control | Delivery risk sits with Auris Nexus once scope is signed off; requirement risk sits with the client at sign-off | Well-defined requirements with limited expected change | [[TYPICAL_DURATION_FIXED]] |
| **Time and materials** | Billed against actual time and resources used, within an agreed rate card | Risk shared; client carries scope-evolution cost, we carry delivery-quality risk | Requirements expected to evolve, or discovery-stage work | [[TYPICAL_DURATION_TM]] |
| **Managed service** | Ongoing operation, maintenance or support for a recurring fee | We carry operational risk within the agreed service level; client carries the cost of the service | Live systems requiring continuity of operation | [[TYPICAL_DURATION_MANAGED]] |
| **Retained advisory** | Ongoing access to advisory capacity for a recurring fee, not tied to a specific deliverable | Client carries decision risk; we carry availability and quality of advice | Sustained strategic or technical input without a discrete build | [[TYPICAL_DURATION_ADVISORY]] |
| **Outcome-based** | Fee structured against an agreed, measurable outcome rather than time or scope | We carry outcome risk directly, aligning our commercial interest with the client's result | Engagements where the outcome can be defined and measured objectively before work begins | [[TYPICAL_DURATION_OUTCOME]] |
