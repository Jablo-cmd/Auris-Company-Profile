# 1.3 Our story

**Standfirst**

> Why the company exists, what it has built, and what it learned in the process.

---

## Copy

Auris Nexus Technologies was established in July 2026 with the aim of providing practical technology
solutions to organisations seeking to improve their digital capabilities and operational efficiency.

The company was founded around a hands-on approach to software development and digital
problem-solving, with experience spanning websites, business applications, workflow systems,
automation and sector-focused platforms.

Auris Nexus operates as a lean technology company, allowing it to work closely with clients and adapt
solutions to specific organisational requirements while developing its capability and portfolio over
time.

**Building our own products alongside client work.**

Since establishment, development has continued in parallel on proprietary platforms — including
Funda360 and Sebetsa Operations — alongside delivered client work. [[PLATFORM_OPERATING_MODEL]]
Building software the company intends to stand behind, not only software commissioned and handed over,
shapes the same discipline applied to every engagement: conservative about complexity, deliberate
about what "finished" actually means, and honest about how far a build has travelled toward genuine,
everyday use.

**Where we are now.**

The company today offers thirteen services across advisory, engineering and managed operation, working
across ten sectors. As a newly established, lean, founder-led company, that range reflects the breadth
of the founder's own technical practice rather than the scale of the organisation delivering it — a
distinction this profile is deliberate about maintaining throughout, most concretely in Part Four's
technical evidence and Part Seven's case studies.

**Milestones.**

| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 2026 | **Establishment** — Auris Nexus Technologies (Pty) Ltd established in July 2026 |
| 2026 | **Digital solutions portfolio** — development and consolidation of Auris Nexus software, web and business-operations solutions |
| 2026 | **Product development** — continued development of proprietary platforms including Funda360, Sebetsa Operations and the Logistics App |
| 2026 | **Client delivery** — delivery of selected digital projects, including websites for Jo Jackson Dance Company and Pro Energy Solutions |

We are candid about the stage we are at. Auris Nexus is a newly established company — the milestones
above span a single year because the company itself is only months old — but its founder brings
hands-on technology delivery experience to it, and the portfolio in Part Seven is real, evidenced work,
not a plan. We do not compete on scale. We compete on direct access to the person doing the work, and
accountability that does not dissipate across a delivery chain because there is no chain to dissipate
across.
