# 1.10 Our operating model

**Standfirst**

> How the business is actually organised — because a supplier's structure determines what it can
> deliver far more reliably than its intentions do.

---

## Copy

Most profiles describe what a company does and omit how it is arranged to do it. For Auris Nexus,
that arrangement is straightforward: the company is currently founder-led, with one person carrying
solution design, development and delivery directly, and specialist capacity contracted in where a
requirement genuinely exceeds what the company holds internally.

### Capability areas

Rather than departments, Auris Nexus organises its offering around five capability areas:

1. **Digital Experience & Web Development**
2. **Custom Software Development**
3. **Business Systems & Automation**
4. **Data & Technology Platforms**
5. **AI & Emerging Technology Integration**

These describe what Auris Nexus is positioned to provide, not five separate teams. Part Two sets out
the thirteen services this spans, and is explicit about which are proven through delivered work and
which are offered as capability.

### Direct accountability

With the founder directly involved in every engagement, there is no internal hand-off between the
person who scopes an engagement and the person who delivers it — the same person does both, from
discovery through to support. That is a structural consequence of the company's current size, not a
policy the company could equally choose to abandon.

### Partner network

Where a client's requirement exceeds what the company holds internally, Auris Nexus is positioned to
contract specialist capacity rather than decline the work outright or present it as in-house
capability it does not have. No specific partner arrangements are named here; any partner engaged on
a project would be declared to the client, with accountability for the outcome remaining with Auris
Nexus.

### What this means for a client

- **You deal directly with the person doing the work** — there is no account layer or delegation
  chain between you and delivery.
- **Capability is stated at the level it is proven.** Where a service is new capability rather than
  demonstrated experience, this document says so rather than implying an established practice.
