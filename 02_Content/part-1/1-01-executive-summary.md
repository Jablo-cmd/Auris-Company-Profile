# 1.1 Executive Overview

**Technology that brings clarity to the way organisations work.**

Auris Nexus Technologies (Pty) Ltd is a South African technology company that designs and builds practical digital systems around the way organisations actually work.

We work with small and medium-sized businesses, professional organisations and educational institutions that need more than an off-the-shelf application, a basic website or disconnected IT services. We understand the operational problem, design the right digital response and build technology that improves how people work, communicate and make decisions.

Our capabilities span **custom business software, client portals, dashboards, professional websites, workflow automation, mobile applications, AI integration and data & business intelligence**.

Our positioning is simple: **Auris Nexus is a technology partner for organisations that want greater transparency, operational control and sustainable growth through technology.**

We begin with the organisation — the process, people, information, bottlenecks and desired outcome — and then determine where technology can create the greatest value.

**Understand → Define → Design → Build → Test → Deploy → Improve**

Our strongest evidence includes Funda360, Sebetsa, LOGIOS OS, CIT Employee & Leave Management and Pro Energy Solutions.

Auris Nexus is 100% South African-owned, registered under **2026/606690/07**, holds **B-BBEE Level 1** status and is based at 140 Linden Road, Sandown, Sandton, Gauteng.

> **We do not need to be the biggest technology company in the room. We need to understand the problem, engineer the solution and remain accountable for the work.**
