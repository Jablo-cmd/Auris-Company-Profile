# 5.0 Project Delivery

## A clear path from business problem to working technology.

Auris Nexus uses a practical delivery lifecycle that gives clients visibility before, during and after development.

### 01 — Understand

We establish the business objective, users, current process, pain points, constraints and desired outcome.

### 02 — Define

We translate the requirement into a practical scope, priorities, assumptions, dependencies and delivery plan.

### 03 — Design

We define workflows, user journeys, data structures, roles, permissions and the technical approach.

### 04 — Build

Development proceeds in controlled increments so that progress can be reviewed and important decisions are made early.

### 05 — Test

We validate functionality, permissions, workflows, responsiveness, data behaviour and relevant technical requirements.

### 06 — Deploy

We prepare the production environment, release the solution and support the transition into operational use.

### 07 — Support and Improve

Where agreed, Auris remains involved through maintenance, issue resolution, enhancements, optimisation and future development.

## How this helps the client

The lifecycle is designed to create:

- Clear expectations
- Visible progress
- Earlier identification of issues
- Better control of scope
- More useful user feedback
- A structured route from launch to improvement

## Engagements can start small

Not every organisation needs a major transformation programme.

Auris can begin with a focused discovery, website, automation, portal, workflow or pilot and use the resulting evidence to determine what should happen next.

That is often the most practical route to technology investment:

**start with the problem → prove the solution → build from there.**
