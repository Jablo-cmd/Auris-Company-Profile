# 5.3 Development

**Standfirst**

> Phase 7 of the lifecycle in 5.0. Where the architecture and design from 5.2 become working software,
> in increments a client can see and respond to before the whole system is complete.

---

## Copy

Development proceeds iteratively wherever the commercial model and requirement allow it (per 2.9),
because a client who can see and respond to working software in increments catches a
misunderstanding while it is still cheap to correct, rather than at final delivery when it is not. Where
an engagement's requirement and contract genuinely call for a more sequential approach, we run one —
the iterative default is a preference stated here as a preference, not a claim that every engagement is
run identically.

Work is tracked against a **definition of done** agreed before development begins, so that "complete"
means something specific and checkable rather than a judgement call made under delivery pressure at
the end. Engineering standards described in 4.1 — code review, testing proportionate to risk,
version control — apply throughout, not as a phase-end activity.

**Definition of done, specific criteria:** [[DEFINITION_OF_DONE]] — confirmed per engagement type,
not stated as one universal standard here.
**Project management framework and certifications held by staff:** [[PM_FRAMEWORK_CERTS]] — no
specific framework (Scrum, PRINCE2, PMP or otherwise) or certification is claimed until confirmed.
