# 5.8 Delivery governance and assurance

**Layout:** Archetype F. RACI matrix and escalation path as the dominant diagram; framing copy in the
sidebar.

---

## Copy

**Standfirst**

> Governance that sits across the delivery team it reviews is not governance — it is self-assessment
> wearing a governance title. At Auris Nexus's current size, independence is built into the checklists
> and defined criteria a gate is checked against, not into a separate governance team — see below for
> what that means in practice today versus as the company grows.

---

### Copy

Auris Nexus is currently a founder-led company, and delivery and review are not carried out by
separate personnel today. The operating principle is this: each gate in the lifecycle described in
5.0 is intended to be checked against written, engagement-specific acceptance criteria agreed with
the client at the planning gate (Phase 3, 5.1), rather than signed off informally on convenience.
That is the standard this section commits to; the specific checklist artefacts that demonstrate it
in practice are produced and agreed per engagement, not asserted as an existing universal template
here.

Where an engagement's scope, risk profile or a client's own governance requirement calls for a
genuinely independent second reviewer — for example, an external security review — that review is
contracted from a named specialist outside the delivery work itself, declared to the client as such,
consistent with the partner-network approach in section 1.10. As Auris Nexus grows beyond its current
founder-led structure, a dedicated, organisationally independent governance function is the intended
next layer of control — not a function that is already staffed.
