## Copy

**Standfirst**

> Governance that sits across the delivery team it reviews is not governance — it is self-assessment
> wearing a governance title. The independence stated in 1.10 is what makes this layer meaningful.

---

### Copy

Every engagement carries a governance layer distinct from the delivery team executing it, per the
operating model in 1.10: the people confirming a gate has been met are not the people whose delivery
timeline benefits from waving it through. This applies at every gate in the lifecycle in 5.0, not only
at project close.

Accountability, responsibility, consultation and information rights for each phase are defined
explicitly, so that "who decides" and "who is informed" are not resolved by whoever happens to be in
the room. Reporting cadence to the client is agreed at the planning gate (Phase 3, 5.1), not left to
arise informally once delivery is under way.
