# 5.8 Delivery governance and assurance

**Layout:** Archetype F. RACI matrix and escalation path as the dominant diagram; framing copy in the
sidebar.

---

## Copy

**Standfirst**

> Accountability that is easy to state and hard to deliver is worth nothing. This page states plainly
> who is accountable at every gate — and, at the company's current size, is honest about what that
> accountability structure can and cannot yet be.

---

### Copy

Every engagement follows the same clear line of accountability, agreed with the client before delivery
begins: what each phase of the lifecycle in 5.0 requires to be signed off, who is answerable for it,
and who is kept informed. This applies at every gate in the lifecycle, not only at project close.

At the company's current size — a single founder, per the operating model in 1.10 — this
accountability is direct rather than distributed across a formal governance function: the founder is
answerable for every gate in every engagement, and there is no separate review layer to describe. This
is stated plainly rather than dressed up, because an evaluator can check headcount against any claim of
independent governance in under a minute. As the company grows, a formal independent review function is
the intended next step in this structure, not something claimed now.

Reporting cadence to the client is agreed at the planning gate (Phase 3, 5.1), not left to arise
informally once delivery is under way, and does not depend on a governance layer to be kept.
