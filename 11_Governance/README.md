## Purpose

This directory contains Auris's initial internal governance policy suite. The policies are designed for a South African technology company and are written to be credible, practical and proportionate to Auris's current operating scale.

These documents are **internal policies, not certifications**. Their existence must not be presented as evidence that Auris holds ISO, COIDA, professional accreditation or another external certification unless that credential is separately verified.

## Approval record

The one-page [Policy Approval & Adoption Record](./Policy-Approval-Adoption-Record.md) is the formal sign-off document for the complete ten-policy pack. It remains pending signature until the Managing Director executes it.

## Adoption process

Before these policies are represented externally as **adopted Auris policies**, the Managing Director should:

1. Review the full pack.
2. Confirm that the procedures match actual Auris operations.
3. Amend any operational details that are not yet implemented.
4. Formally approve the policies.
5. Record the approval date and version.
6. Implement any operational controls required by the policies.
7. Review the pack at least annually or after material changes.

## Important implementation items

The policies deliberately do not claim that Auris currently has:

- ISO certification;
- COIDA registration;
- a formal security certification;
- a certified business continuity management system;
- a large compliance department;
- guaranteed service levels beyond signed contracts; or
- any other credential not independently verified.

For POPIA/PAIA governance, Auris should separately confirm its Information Officer registration and PAIA documentation requirements. The Information Regulator provides official services for Information Officer registration, PAIA manuals, POPIA self-assessment and security-compromise reporting.

## Document control

Future revisions should increment the version number and record the reason for change. Superseded versions should be retained according to Auris's records-management requirements.
