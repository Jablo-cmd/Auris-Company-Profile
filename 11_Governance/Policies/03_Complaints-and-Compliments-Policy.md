# Auris Nexus Technologies — Complaints & Compliments Policy

**Policy ID:** AUR-POL-003  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 18 September 2026  
**Policy owner:** Managing Director  
**Review cycle:** At least annually

## 1. Purpose
Auris welcomes feedback and provides a fair, professional process for receiving, investigating and resolving complaints.

## 2. Scope
This policy covers complaints from clients, prospective clients, suppliers, partners and other stakeholders concerning Auris services, conduct, communication, billing, delivery or support.

## 3. Principles
Complaints will be handled respectfully, objectively, confidentially where appropriate and without retaliation against a person who raises a genuine concern.

## 4. How to complain
A complaint may be submitted through the normal Auris business contact channel or directly to management. The complainant should provide enough information to identify the issue, relevant dates, affected service and desired resolution where applicable.

## 5. Acknowledgement and assessment
Auris should acknowledge a material complaint within a reasonable period. Management will assess severity, urgency, contractual obligations and whether investigation by another person is appropriate.

## 6. Investigation
Auris may review project records, communications, tickets, invoices, technical evidence and relevant agreements. The investigator should distinguish facts, allegations and unresolved questions.

## 7. Resolution
Possible outcomes include correction of work, clarification, service recovery, agreed remediation, commercial adjustment where justified, or a finding that no corrective action is required.

## 8. Escalation
Where the complainant is dissatisfied, the matter may be escalated to the Managing Director. Contractual dispute-resolution procedures remain applicable where they exist.

## 9. Records
Material complaints and their outcomes should be recorded, including date, issue, investigation, actions and closure.

## 10. Compliments
Positive feedback may be recorded and, with appropriate permission, used as a testimonial. Auris will not publish a client's name, logo or quotation without appropriate authorisation.

## 11. Serious matters
Allegations involving fraud, corruption, discrimination, harassment, security compromise, unlawful conduct or significant safety risk must be escalated immediately.
