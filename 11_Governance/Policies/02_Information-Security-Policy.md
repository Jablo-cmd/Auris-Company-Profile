# Auris Nexus Technologies — Information Security Policy

**Policy ID:** AUR-POL-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 18 September 2026  
**Policy owner:** Managing Director  
**Review cycle:** At least annually

## 1. Purpose
Auris protects information and technology resources against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse and disruption.

## 2. Scope
The policy applies to Auris systems, source code, repositories, cloud services, devices, credentials, client information, documentation, communications and third-party services used for Auris work.

## 3. Security objectives
Auris will protect:
- confidentiality of sensitive information;
- integrity of systems and records; and
- availability of systems required for service delivery.

## 4. Access control
Access must be based on business need and least privilege. Shared credentials should be avoided. Privileged access must be limited and reviewed when roles or engagements change.

Personnel must use strong authentication and multi-factor authentication where supported and appropriate.

## 5. Credentials and secrets
Passwords, API keys, tokens, private keys and similar secrets must not be committed to public repositories or embedded in client-facing source code. Secrets must be stored using appropriate secret-management facilities.

Suspected credential compromise must be reported immediately and affected credentials rotated as appropriate.

## 6. Devices and endpoints
Auris devices used for business activity should use supported operating systems, current security updates, screen locking and appropriate endpoint protection. Lost or stolen devices must be reported promptly.

## 7. Software and development security
Production software must use reasonable secure-development practices appropriate to its risk. Changes should be reviewed, tested and traceable. Dependencies should be monitored and security-sensitive changes treated with additional care.

## 8. Backups and recovery
Critical business and project information should have appropriate backup or recoverability arrangements. Backup effectiveness should be tested where practical.

## 9. Security incidents
Security incidents must be reported immediately. Auris will assess, contain, investigate, recover and document material incidents. Where personal information is involved, the POPIA & Privacy Policy applies.

## 10. Third parties
Third parties with access to Auris or client information should be assessed according to risk and bound by suitable confidentiality, security and data-processing obligations.

## 11. Physical security
Confidential information must not be left exposed in public or uncontrolled environments. Physical access to equipment and records should be reasonably controlled.

## 12. Security awareness
Personnel and contractors are expected to understand phishing, social engineering, credential protection, safe handling of client data and secure use of systems.

## 13. Exceptions
Any material exception to this policy must be documented, risk-assessed and approved by management.

## 14. Policy status
This policy establishes Auris's internal security baseline. It does not represent certification against ISO 27001, NIST or another external standard unless Auris separately obtains and verifies such certification.
