# Auris Nexus Technologies — Code of Conduct & Ethics

**Policy ID:** AUR-POL-008  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Proposed effective date:** Upon formal approval  
**Policy owner:** Managing Director  
**Review cycle:** At least annually

## 1. Purpose
Auris conducts business with professionalism, honesty, respect and accountability.

## 2. Expected conduct
Auris personnel and representatives are expected to:
- act honestly and professionally;
- protect confidential information;
- respect clients, colleagues, suppliers and the public;
- deliver work within agreed scope and communicate limitations;
- avoid misleading claims about Auris capability, credentials or experience;
- maintain accurate business records; and
- comply with applicable laws and contractual obligations.

## 3. Respectful workplace
Auris does not tolerate unlawful discrimination, harassment, intimidation, threats or abusive conduct in connection with its work.

## 4. Confidentiality
Confidential company and client information must only be used for authorised business purposes and must be protected from unauthorised disclosure.

## 5. Conflicts
Personal interests that could influence business decisions must be disclosed and appropriately managed.

## 6. Intellectual property
Personnel must respect Auris, client and third-party intellectual-property rights and must not knowingly misuse protected material.

## 7. Accuracy and claims
Auris communications must distinguish verified facts from plans, capabilities and future intentions. Certifications, awards, client relationships, statistics and testimonials must not be represented as current unless verified.

## 8. Reporting
Concerns about misconduct should be reported to management. Serious concerns may be escalated to appropriate authorities where required.

## 9. Consequences
Breaches may lead to corrective action, removal from duties, contractual remedies or disciplinary action, subject to applicable law.
