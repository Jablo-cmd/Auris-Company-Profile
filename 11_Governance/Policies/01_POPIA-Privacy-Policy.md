# Auris Nexus Technologies — POPIA & Privacy Policy

**Policy ID:** AUR-POL-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Proposed effective date:** Upon formal approval  
**Policy owner:** Managing Director  
**Approval authority:** Managing Director  
**Review cycle:** At least annually and whenever material legal, operational or technology changes occur

## 1. Purpose
Auris Nexus Technologies (Pty) Ltd ("Auris") processes personal information in the course of delivering technology services, communicating with clients and suppliers, managing its business and supporting its products. This policy establishes Auris's approach to lawful, responsible and secure processing of personal information.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to Auris personnel, contractors and service providers who process personal information on behalf of Auris or in connection with Auris operations.

## 3. Principles
Auris will seek to process personal information lawfully and reasonably; collect it for specific legitimate purposes; avoid excessive collection; maintain reasonable accuracy; retain information only as long as justified; respect data-subject rights; and apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards.

## 4. Collection and use
Auris may process information required for contracting, service delivery, support, billing, recruitment, supplier management, security, communications and legal obligations. Personal information must not be collected merely because it is available.

Where consent is relied upon, it must be appropriately obtained and records maintained. Where another lawful basis applies, processing must be limited to that basis and purpose.

## 5. Access and disclosure
Access to personal information is restricted according to business need and role. Information may be disclosed to authorised service providers, professional advisers, authorities or other recipients where legally permitted, contractually required or otherwise justified.

Third-party operators handling personal information for Auris must be appropriately assessed and subject to suitable contractual confidentiality and security obligations.

## 6. Data-subject requests
Auris will maintain reasonable procedures for handling requests concerning access, correction, deletion, objection or other rights available under applicable law. Requests must be escalated to the designated Information Officer or responsible management function.

## 7. Security compromises
Suspected or confirmed personal-information compromises must be reported internally immediately. Auris will assess the incident, contain it, preserve relevant evidence, document decisions and make notifications required by applicable law.

POPIA requires appropriate technical and organisational measures and provides for notification of certain security compromises. Auris will follow the Information Regulator's applicable reporting process where required.

## 8. Retention and disposal
Personal information must be retained only for as long as required for its stated purpose, contractual/legal obligations, legitimate business needs or dispute resolution. Secure disposal must be used when retention is no longer justified.

## 9. Children and special personal information
Auris will apply additional care when processing children's information or special personal information and will only process such information where an appropriate legal basis and safeguards exist.

## 10. Responsibilities
All personnel are responsible for protecting personal information. Management is responsible for implementing controls, assigning responsibilities and reviewing this policy. The Information Officer, once formally designated and registered where required, will oversee applicable privacy compliance activities.

## 11. Records
Auris will maintain appropriate records of material privacy decisions, incidents, requests, operator arrangements and policy reviews.

## 12. Breach of policy
Failure to comply may result in access restrictions, corrective action, contractual consequences or disciplinary action, subject to applicable law.

## 13. Legal note
This policy is an internal governance document and does not replace legal advice, Auris's PAIA Manual, client contracts, privacy notices or applicable legislation.
