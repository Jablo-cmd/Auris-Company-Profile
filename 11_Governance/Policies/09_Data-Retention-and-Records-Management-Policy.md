# Auris Nexus Technologies — Data Retention & Records Management Policy

**Policy ID:** AUR-POL-009  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Proposed effective date:** Upon formal approval  
**Policy owner:** Managing Director  
**Review cycle:** At least annually

## 1. Purpose
Auris maintains records needed to operate the business, deliver services, meet legal and contractual obligations and demonstrate accountability, while avoiding unnecessary retention.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to business records, contracts, financial records, project documentation, source code, support records, personnel records, supplier records, communications and personal information held by Auris.

## 3. Classification
Records should be treated according to sensitivity, including:
- Public;
- Internal;
- Confidential; and
- Restricted.

Personal information and security credentials require appropriate protection regardless of classification.

## 4. Retention
Retention periods must be determined by the purpose of the record, applicable law, contractual obligations, tax/accounting requirements, dispute or investigation needs and operational value.

Where a specific legal or contractual retention period applies, that requirement takes precedence.

## 5. Secure disposal
When records are no longer required, Auris should securely delete, destroy or anonymise them as appropriate. Disposal must not occur where a legal hold, investigation, dispute or contractual requirement requires preservation.

## 6. Source code and technical records
Source repositories, deployment records, architecture documentation and security-sensitive technical records should be retained according to project and operational requirements and protected against unauthorised alteration or disclosure.

## 7. Personal information
Retention of personal information must be consistent with Auris's POPIA & Privacy Policy. Personal information must not be retained indefinitely merely because storage is inexpensive.

## 8. Legal holds
Management may issue a legal or investigation hold requiring relevant records to be preserved.

## 9. Records integrity
Material business records should be maintained in a form that supports authenticity, traceability and reasonable retrieval.

## 10. Review
Management should periodically review storage locations and retention practices and remove obsolete information where appropriate.
