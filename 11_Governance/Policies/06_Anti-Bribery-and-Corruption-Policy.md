# Auris Nexus Technologies — Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy

**Policy ID:** AUR-POL-006  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 18 September 2026  
**Policy owner:** Managing Director  
**Review cycle:** At least annually

## 1. Zero tolerance
Auris prohibits bribery, corruption, fraud, kickbacks and improper payments in connection with its business.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to directors, employees, contractors, representatives, suppliers and anyone acting on behalf of Auris.

## 3. Prohibited conduct
No person acting for Auris may:
- offer, promise, give, request or accept an improper advantage;
- make facilitation payments where prohibited by law or policy;
- manipulate procurement or tender processes;
- falsify records to conceal a payment or benefit;
- provide an undisclosed kickback or commission; or
- use an intermediary to do something Auris personnel are prohibited from doing directly.

## 4. Gifts and hospitality
Reasonable business hospitality must never be used to improperly influence a decision. Gifts or hospitality involving public officials, procurement decisions or active tenders require particular caution and, where material, management approval.

## 5. Public-sector engagements
Auris will comply with applicable South African anti-corruption laws and procurement requirements when engaging with public bodies. No payment or benefit may be offered to improperly influence an official or procurement outcome.

## 6. Books and records
Business records must accurately reflect transactions. Off-book funds, false invoices and disguised payments are prohibited.

## 7. Reporting
Suspected bribery, corruption or fraud must be reported promptly to management. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## 8. Non-retaliation
Auris will not knowingly retaliate against a person who raises a genuine concern in good faith.

## 9. Consequences
Confirmed misconduct may lead to removal from an engagement, contractual remedies, disciplinary action and/or referral to appropriate authorities, subject to applicable law.

## 10. Legal framework
This policy is intended to support compliance with applicable South African law, including the Prevention and Combating of Corrupt Activities Act 12 of 2004. It does not replace legal advice.

**Approved by:** Managing Director, Auris Nexus Technologies (Pty) Ltd
