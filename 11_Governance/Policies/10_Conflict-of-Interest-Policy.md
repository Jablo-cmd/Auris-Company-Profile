# Auris Nexus Technologies — Conflict of Interest Policy

**Policy ID:** AUR-POL-010  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Proposed effective date:** Upon formal approval  
**Policy owner:** Managing Director  
**Review cycle:** At least annually

## 1. Purpose
Auris makes business decisions objectively and seeks to prevent personal interests from improperly influencing company decisions.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to directors, employees, contractors, representatives and anyone participating in Auris decisions.

## 3. What constitutes a conflict
A conflict may arise where a person's personal, family, financial, employment, ownership or other interests could influence, or reasonably appear to influence, an Auris decision.

Conflicts may be actual, potential or perceived.

## 4. Disclosure
A person who becomes aware of a relevant conflict must disclose it promptly to the Managing Director or appropriate decision-maker.

## 5. Management of conflicts
Depending on the circumstances, management may:
- require the person to recuse themselves;
- assign an independent decision-maker;
- obtain additional quotations or evidence;
- impose approval controls;
- document the rationale for the decision; or
- prohibit the transaction.

## 6. Procurement and suppliers
Auris personnel must disclose personal relationships or financial interests involving prospective or existing suppliers, clients or competitors where those interests could affect an Auris decision.

## 7. Gifts and benefits
Gifts, hospitality, discounts or benefits that could influence an Auris decision must not be accepted. The Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy also applies.

## 8. Related-party transactions
Transactions involving related parties require disclosure and appropriate approval before commitment.

## 9. Records
Material conflict disclosures and management decisions should be documented.

## 10. Good-faith disclosure
A person who discloses a genuine conflict in good faith will not be penalised merely for making the disclosure.

## 11. Breach
Failure to disclose a material conflict may result in corrective or disciplinary action and, where applicable, contractual or legal consequences.
