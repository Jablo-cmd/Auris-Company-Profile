# Auris Nexus Technologies — Health & Safety Policy

**Policy ID:** AUR-POL-004  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 18 September 2026  
**Policy owner:** Managing Director  
**Review cycle:** At least annually

## 1. Purpose
Auris is committed to conducting its activities in a manner that reasonably protects employees, contractors, clients, visitors and members of the public from foreseeable health and safety risks.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to Auris personnel and contractors during Auris work, including office activities, client-site visits, installations, demonstrations and project-related travel.

## 3. Risk management
Work-related hazards should be identified before higher-risk activities. Controls should be selected according to the nature and severity of the risk and applicable client-site requirements.

## 4. Client sites
Auris personnel must comply with reasonable and lawful site rules, induction requirements, access controls and safety instructions applicable to client premises.

## 5. Equipment and installations
Equipment must be handled according to manufacturer guidance and applicable site requirements. Electrical, construction or other specialised work must not be undertaken outside Auris's competence or authorisation.

## 6. Incidents
Accidents, injuries, unsafe conditions and near misses must be reported promptly to management and, where applicable, the client. Material incidents must be documented and investigated sufficiently to identify corrective actions.

## 7. Emergency response
Personnel should follow the emergency procedures of the premises they are visiting. Where Auris controls a workplace or activity, appropriate emergency arrangements must be maintained.

## 8. Fitness and conduct
Personnel must not undertake work while impaired in a manner that creates a safety risk. Unsafe conduct may result in removal from the work activity.

## 9. Contractors
Contractors must meet applicable safety requirements and remain responsible for the lawful and safe performance of their work.

## 10. Legal framework
Auris will comply with applicable South African occupational health and safety requirements relevant to its activities, including the Occupational Health and Safety Act where applicable.

**Important:** This policy does not state that Auris is registered with COIDA or holds any health-and-safety certification. Those credentials will only be stated if separately verified.

**Approved by:** Managing Director, Auris Nexus Technologies (Pty) Ltd
