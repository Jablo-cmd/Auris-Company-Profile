# Auris Nexus Technologies — Business Continuity Policy

**Policy ID:** AUR-POL-007  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 18 September 2026  
**Policy owner:** Managing Director  
**Review cycle:** At least annually and after a material incident

## 1. Purpose
Auris seeks to maintain essential business and client-service capabilities during disruptions and to recover critical activities within a reasonable period.

## 2. Scope
This policy covers disruptions affecting people, premises, devices, connectivity, cloud services, source repositories, critical suppliers, data or other resources required for material operations.

## 3. Priority services
During a disruption, Auris will prioritise:
1. safety and protection of people;
2. protection of client and company information;
3. critical client commitments;
4. restoration of essential systems and communications; and
5. normal operations and improvement.

## 4. Continuity measures
Auris should maintain appropriate redundancy, backups, documented contacts, access to critical credentials, recoverable project information and alternative communication methods appropriate to its scale and risk.

## 5. Incident leadership
The Managing Director or delegated responsible person coordinates material continuity events, establishes priorities and communicates with affected stakeholders.

## 6. Client communication
Where a disruption materially affects a client commitment, Auris will communicate relevant facts, expected impact and recovery actions as soon as reasonably practical.

## 7. Recovery
Recovery activities should restore the most critical services first. Material incidents should be reviewed afterwards to identify improvements.

## 8. Testing
Continuity arrangements should be reviewed periodically. Important backup and recovery mechanisms should be tested where practical.

## 9. Third parties
Critical third-party dependencies should be identified and alternative arrangements considered where reasonable.

## 10. Limitations
This policy does not claim that Auris currently maintains a certified business-continuity management system or guaranteed recovery time for every service. Client-specific recovery commitments must be stated in the applicable contract or SLA.
