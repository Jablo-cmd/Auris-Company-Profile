# Auris Nexus Technologies — Procurement & Supplier Policy

**Policy ID:** AUR-POL-005  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Proposed effective date:** Upon formal approval  
**Policy owner:** Managing Director  
**Review cycle:** At least annually

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes a fair, documented and commercially responsible approach to purchasing goods and services and selecting suppliers.

## 2. Principles
Auris procurement should consider:
- value for money;
- suitability and quality;
- supplier capability;
- reliability and delivery;
- total cost of ownership;
- legal and contractual requirements;
- information-security and privacy risk;
- conflicts of interest; and
- ethical conduct.

## 3. Supplier selection
Where practical and proportionate to the purchase, Auris should compare suitable suppliers or obtain competitive quotations. For specialised, urgent or low-value purchases, a formal multi-quote process may not be practical; the reason should be recorded where material.

## 4. Due diligence
Higher-risk suppliers may be checked for identity, ownership, tax/compliance status, capability, references, security practices and relevant certifications.

## 5. Approval
Material purchases should receive appropriate management approval before commitment. No person should approve their own undisclosed personal benefit.

## 6. Conflicts of interest
Actual, potential or perceived conflicts must be disclosed. Management will determine appropriate mitigation, including recusal or independent review.

## 7. Supplier conduct
Auris expects suppliers to act lawfully, honestly and professionally and to protect confidential information. Bribery, fraud, kickbacks and improper inducements are prohibited.

## 8. Records
Material procurement decisions should retain appropriate quotations, approvals, purchase records, supplier details and relevant correspondence.

## 9. Related parties
Transactions involving related parties or persons with a personal interest require disclosure and management approval.

## 10. Sustainability and transformation
Where commercially appropriate, Auris may consider local capability, responsible sourcing, transformation and supplier-development objectives without compromising legal, technical, security or quality requirements.
